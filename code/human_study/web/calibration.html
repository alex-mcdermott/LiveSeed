How to rate the images

You will first see images one at a time, each with the text prompt that produced it. Give every image two scores from 1 to 5. Afterwards, you will choose your favourite images from a few sets.

The two scores are independent. A beautiful image can score 5 for quality and 1 for alignment if it shows the wrong thing. An image can score 5 for alignment and 2 for quality if everything requested is there but it looks mangled. Score each image on its own, using the descriptions below.

Prompt alignment: did it follow the prompt?
5All subjects, attributes, counts and spatial relations are exactly as requested.
4The core is correct, but one minor detail is missing or slightly wrong.
3The main subject is present, but there is a serious error: a subject is missing, the count is wrong, or attributes are swapped.
2Only loosely related: it has the general theme or one requested object, but ignores the main structure.
1Nothing to do with the prompt.
Image quality: ignore the prompt, how well made is it?
5Flawless: no AI artefacts, correct anatomy and perspective, coherent textures.
4Good: very minor imperfections that you only notice on close inspection.
3Acceptable: noticeable artefacts, but objects are recognisable and intact.
2Poor: severe structural problems or heavy artefacts, such as mangled limbs or severe blur.
1Unusable: objects fused into impossible shapes, or a corrupted image of unrecognisable forms or noise.

Part 2: choose your favourites

You have finished rating. Now you will see 4 sets of 10 new images. Each set was made from one prompt.

Imagine you asked an AI to make an image from this prompt. Which three would you keep? Click your favourite first, then your second, then your third. This is your own preference, so there are no right answers.

Click your 1st, 2nd and 3rd choice, in that order. Click a chosen image again to clear it. Use "Enlarge" for a closer look.
